BoldMind clause playbook

This is where historical redlines become a repeatable review standard.

Clause Preferred position Default action
Confidentiality term 2-3 years, trade secrets carved out if needed Shorten if indefinite
Non-solicit Limited to named deal team and target employees Narrow scope
Residual knowledge No broad residuals that weaken confidentiality Delete or constrain
Jurisdiction Aligned with deal documents or acceptable neutral venue Escalate if mismatch

Review result

Partner sees what changed, why it matters, and the recommended fallback.

3 flags
High
Non-solicit too broad

Draft restricts hiring across all affiliates for 24 months. BoldMind should narrow to people directly involved in the process.

Suggested edit: Limit non-solicit to named management and deal team contacts for 12 months.
Medium
Indefinite confidentiality term

Current language has no sunset for all information. This is acceptable only for trade secrets.

Suggested edit: Set 3-year term for ordinary confidential information.
Medium
Residual knowledge carve-out

Residuals clause could allow use of information remembered by team members.

Suggested edit: Delete clause or constrain it so it cannot override confidentiality duties.

Pilot notes

Product thesis

BoldMind already uses AI across screening, diligence, models, research, legal review, and reporting. The next step is not another AI chat. The next step is a controlled workflow layer around the fund's existing work.

Problem AI work is valuable but fragmented across manual uploads, prompts, files, and approvals.
Solution Attach every AI output to a deal, document, owner, task, and approval state.
First wedge Deal screening and NDA review: high-frequency, easy to understand, and partner-relevant.
