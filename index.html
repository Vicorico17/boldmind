AI variance commentary

Generated from the latest KPI pack and management update.

Watch
What changed

Revenue reached USD 2.0m in Q1'26, 2% above budget and 10% above prior year. Segment X was the main positive driver.

Why it changed

Segment X improved 3% versus budget after two enterprise customers expanded usage earlier than planned. Segment Y stayed flat.

Key risks

EBITDA is still 6% below budget because the mix shifted toward lower-margin implementation revenue and receivables remain elevated.

Assumptions

The revenue plan looks realistic if Segment X retention holds, but margin recovery depends on moving implementation work into recurring contracts.

Follow-up questions
  • Which Segment X customers drove the 3% outperformance?
  • How much of the Q1 revenue uplift is recurring versus implementation revenue?
  • What collection actions bring cash conversion back to budget?
